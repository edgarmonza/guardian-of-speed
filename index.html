About Nicolas.

The Custodian

Nicolas Navarro founded Guardian of Speed with a single conviction: the world's finest automobiles deserve a custodian, not a caretaker.

From the engine bays of Munich to the winding roads of the Alps, Nicolas built his expertise hands-on. Today, he oversees every aspect of collection management — from technical hibernation to world-class event logistics — ensuring that each vehicle under his guardianship is not merely preserved, but ready.

Ready to drive. Ready to impress. Ready for the moment.
