Our Services.

Service Architecture

Four custodial pillars. Each stands alone. Together, they form the silent apparatus behind every fully-readied machine. We do not just buy cars. We curate legacies.

01 / IV

The Asset Guardian.

Technical Hibernation · Technische Einlagerung

Beyond storage. Technical preservation, fluid and battery management, engine cycling, and European registration bureaucracy — H-Plates, TÜV, all of it. Your assets don't sleep; they wait.

  • Climate-controlled long-term storage
  • Fluid management and engine cycling programs
  • Battery conditioning and electrical preservation
  • European registration: H-Plates, TÜV, bureaucracy
  • Documentation and service history management
02 / IV

The Trusted Advisor.

Acquisition & Curation · Akquisition & Kuration

Pre-purchase inspections, technical vetting, and negotiation for cars anywhere in the world. Access to off-market inventory. We curate the best artisans — for bodywork, for mechanics, for paint — and we oversee the process until perfection.

  • Pre-purchase inspections and technical vetting
  • Global negotiation and off-market access
  • Restoration oversight to concours standards
  • Original parts sourcing through our network
  • Valuation and insurance advisory
03 / IV

The Experience Architect.

World-Class Event Logistics · Erstklassige Eventlogistik

Seamless logistics for Mille Miglia, Villa d'Este, Carrera Nuvolari, Pebble Beach, Retromobile. We manage the transport, the mechanics, and the reality — so you simply arrive and drive.

  • Enclosed international transport with real-time tracking
  • Event registration, logistics, and on-site support
  • Pre-event preparation and post-event care
  • Concours preparation and judging consultation
  • Complete discretion guaranteed
04 / IV

Driver Concierge.

On-Rally Vehicle Care · Rallye-Fahrzeugbetreuung

During events like the 1,600 km Mille Miglia — oil levels, technical checks, daily readiness. In-car hydration, navigation setup, charging. You simply drive.

  • Daily vehicle readiness checks during events
  • Oil levels, tyre pressures, fluid monitoring
  • In-car preparation: hydration, navigation, charging
  • Technical support vehicle following the rally
  • Emergency mechanical response
